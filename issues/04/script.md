# SUPER DUKIE — ISSUE 4
## Complete comic script — 22 pages

This script follows the locked Issue 4 structure. Panel counts are deliberate; large and silent panels should retain their breathing room. Dialogue below is lettering text. Visual directions and continuity notes are production instructions, not captions.

## Illustration continuity

- Use approved existing likenesses for Duke, Grandma, Shannon, Magnus, and the named farm animals. Do not invent replacement designs. Duke retains his actual proportions and markings. Ordinary Duke has ordinary ears; transformed Duke's ears integrate into his cape, leaving no separate visible ears and no normal collar.
- Grandma's recovery appearance carries the consequences of Issue 3: supported upper body, bandaged injury, exhausted complexion. Her boots remain present beside her bed. In the dream, she is healthy and wears her established farm clothes and boots. In the flashback, preserve her likeness beneath a coat and retain her boots. Glasses remain consistent with the approved reference.
- Animal dialogue is understood by the animals. Shannon hears ordinary animal noises. Duke never speaks human dialogue where Shannon can hear him. Shannon does not witness a transformation.
- The Creator and Doctor Silas Magnus are different people. The Creator remains tall and lean, in his charcoal coat, rigid metal collar, with uncovered lined face and technological gauntlet. Magnus uses his established design; his present-day clothing and equipment remain consistent throughout.
- Harvester geography: a tall central extraction column descends vertically into a bore. Six articulated legs surround it, numbered clockwise when viewed from above. Each carries a large readable numeral. Leg 1 points toward the Creator's elevated control platform; Leg 4 points toward the woods and Duke's approach. Establish these positions on Page 4 and do not move the numbering between shots.
- The low regulator housing sits between Legs 4 and 5. A translucent containment reservoir occupies the upper machine, with a segmented level indicator. Automatic sealing collars surround the energy conduits. Three repulsion emitters are spaced around the waist; after each discharge their bright rings go dark, then refill in three visible segments before firing again.
- A maintenance network socket is beneath Leg 4's chassis connection. Magnus works from cover outside the leg's sweep, using a cable to this socket and an earpiece link to Duke. The Creator's separate control platform has a rear evacuation ramp.
- An armored transfer line exits the harvester between Legs 1 and 2 and follows a service trench toward the forest facility. Its gate assembly is low on that side: broad round pressure housing, adjacent narrow rack with a captive sliding bolt, and a lifting lever. On Page 20 the rack is jammed. On Page 21 Duke removes the bolt and lifts the lever; he does not smash the pressure housing or puncture containment.
- Action damage accumulates. The regulator remains destroyed; the reservoir keeps filling; damaged conduits stay visibly sealed. The final transfer damages the facility but leaves a separate auxiliary work bay operational. No completed robot dog appears in this issue.
